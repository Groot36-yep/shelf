Shelf

Renames your movie and TV files so Plex recognizes them.

Download for Windows

Free. Windows 10 and 11. Windows may say “unknown publisher”: click More info → Run anyway.

Private by design

  • Your videos never leave your computer, except to your own Plex server when you send them. Only a cleaned-up title is looked up.
  • No accounts, tracking, analytics or update checks.
  • Your TMDB key is stored encrypted with your Windows account.
  • Never overwrites a file, and Undo puts everything back.
